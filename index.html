A Peaceful Family Resort in Kushalnagar, Coorg

Nestled near the quiet hamlet of Chikkathur in Kushalnagar, Dreamy Vacations is a dedicated family and group getaway designed for pure relaxation. Set amidst Coorg's legendary greenery, our property combines the peaceful serenity of the Western Ghats with modern comfort and warm Kodava hospitality.

Whether you are planning a weekend family escape, an annual reunion, or a leisure holiday with close friends, our resort offers private double and multiple-bed accommodations tailored for every group size. Spend your afternoons relaxing by our crystal-clear outdoor swimming pool, enjoying music under our rhythmic rain dance shower, or gathering around an evening campfire under starry skies.

Places to Visit Near Kushalnagar & Coorg Sightseeing

Dreamy Vacations is situated minutes away from Coorg's top tourist destinations, saving you travel hours and making day trips convenient.

Namdroling Monastery (Golden Temple) 6 km away (12 mins drive). Marvel at towering 40ft golden statues and Tibetan murals in Bylakuppe.
Kaveri Nisargadhama Forest Island 4 km away (8 mins drive). Walk across the bamboo hanging bridge and explore deer parks by the Kaveri River.
Dubare Elephant Camp 14 km away (20 mins drive). Participate in morning elephant bathing and river rafting on the Kaveri.
Harangi Dam & Reservoir 9 km away (15 mins drive). Scenic reservoir garden and majestic water vistas surrounded by forested hills.
