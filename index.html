Places to Visit Near Kushalnagar & Coorg Travel Guides

Dreamy Vacations is situated minutes away from eastern Coorg's cultural and river landmarks and 30 km east of Madikeri's hills and waterfalls.

Namdroling Monastery (Golden Temple) ~6 km away (12 mins drive). Home to 60-foot and 58-foot gilded Buddha statues and Tibetan murals in Bylakuppe.
Kaveri Nisargadhama Forest Island ~4 km away (8 mins drive). Cross the bamboo hanging bridge and walk shaded deer-park trails by the Cauvery River.
Dubare Elephant Camp ~14 km away (20 mins drive). Morning elephant bathing sessions and seasonal still-water rafting on the Cauvery.
Harangi Dam & Reservoir ~9 km away (15 mins drive). Scenic masonry dam, landscaped garden lawns, and quiet forested waterways.
